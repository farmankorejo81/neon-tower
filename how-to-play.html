NEON TOWER

How to Play Neon Tower

Neon Tower is a timing and alignment game. Move your attention with the travelling block, choose when to drop it, and keep as much width as possible in your growing tower.

Start a round

Open the Neon Tower game and select Play. A coloured block moves horizontally above the stack. It travels back and forth, and its movement becomes faster as the tower grows. You do not need to register or install anything.

Controls

Desktop: press Space or Enter, or click the game area while playing.
Touchscreen: tap the game area while playing.
Sound: use the Sound button to turn audio feedback on or off.

Understand each placement

  1. Watch the moving block. Notice which direction it is travelling and where its edges line up with the top platform.
  2. Choose the drop moment. Tap, click or press a supported key to place the block.
  3. Keep the overlap. The game keeps only the horizontal area shared by the moving block and the platform below it.
  4. Adapt to the new width. If the placement is off-centre, the next block will be narrower.
  5. Continue or restart. A complete miss ends the round. Play Again starts a fresh tower.

Perfect placements and combos

A placement close to exact alignment is recognised as Perfect. Consecutive Perfect placements increase the combo count and trigger a visual effect. A combo is a reward for consistent alignment; it does not replace the basic rule that the blocks must overlap.

How the score works

Each successful placement adds one point. Your current score appears during the round. When the round ends, the game shows the score and the best score recorded in that browser. The best score is stored using browser local storage when available. It is not a global leaderboard, and it may not transfer to another browser or device.

Timing tips

  • Use the left and right edges of the top platform as visual reference points.
  • Watch the direction of travel before deciding when to tap.
  • After a partial overlap, focus on the smaller target rather than the original platform width.
  • As movement speeds up, make a deliberate decision instead of waiting for a perfect-looking pause.
  • Try several short rounds to learn the movement pattern and build your own timing rhythm.

Common questions

Why did part of my block disappear?

The game removes the part that extends beyond the platform below. Only the overlapping section becomes the next level of the tower.

Why did the round end?

The moving block did not overlap the tower when it was dropped, so there was no section that could be added.

Will my best score always be saved?

The game attempts to save it in local storage. Browser privacy settings, private browsing, clearing site data or changing devices can affect local records.

Can I play without sound?

Yes. Sound is optional. Turn it off with the Sound button; visual feedback and scoring remain available.

Report a problem

If the game does not respond, first try refreshing the page and using an up-to-date browser. If the issue continues, visit the Contact page and describe your device, browser and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords or other private information in a public issue.
