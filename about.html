NEON TOWER

About Neon Tower

A small browser game built around one clear challenge: judge the movement, choose your moment and keep your tower standing.

Neon Tower is a free, timing-based block stacking game. A block moves across the screen and the player chooses when to drop it onto the stack. The aim is to build a high tower while preserving as much width as possible. The game is designed for short sessions and can be played in a modern desktop or mobile browser without creating an account.

What makes the challenge interesting?

Precision

Every drop matters. When the moving block is not perfectly aligned, only the overlapping part remains. That makes the next placement more demanding.

Progressive difficulty

As the tower grows, the moving block becomes faster. Players need to adjust their timing instead of relying on one fixed rhythm.

Personal progress

The game can remember a best score in the current browser using local storage. It is a personal device-level record, not a shared online leaderboard.

Player choice

Use touch, mouse or keyboard input, and turn sound feedback on or off with the Sound control.

How a round works

  1. Start the game from the home page.
  2. Observe the moving block and the edges of the top platform.
  3. Drop the block when you judge the alignment is best.
  4. Keep building while adapting to any reduction in platform width.
  5. After a missed placement, review your score and start another round.

For a complete explanation of the controls, overlap rule, scoring and practical timing advice, read the Neon Tower game guide.

Project approach

Neon Tower is an independently maintained browser-game project. The aim is to keep the experience straightforward: load the game, understand the rules quickly and play without an account or installation. The information pages are intended to explain the actual game and its limitations rather than promise features that are not available.

Feedback and improvements

Found a reproducible bug or have an idea that would make the game easier to understand? Use the Contact and feedback page. Reports that include the browser, device type and steps to reproduce a problem are especially useful. Please do not publish private information.
