NEON TOWER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains the information that may be handled when you visit or play Neon Tower at neon-tower-ivory.vercel.app. Neon Tower is a browser-based game and does not ask players to register for an account.

1. Information used by the game

The game uses browser local storage, when available, to remember game preferences such as sound and a best score. This information stays in the browser on the device where it was saved; the game code does not send those saved preferences to a Neon Tower account or game server. Clearing browser site data, using private browsing or switching devices may remove or separate these records.

2. Hosting and basic technical data

The website is delivered through a third-party hosting platform. Like many websites, the hosting provider and related infrastructure may process technical request information (for example, IP address, browser details, requested pages and security or diagnostic logs) to deliver, protect and maintain the site. The exact information and retention periods are controlled by the relevant provider. Review the hosting provider's own privacy information for details.

3. Advertising and cookies

The website includes Google AdSense's advertising script. Whether advertisements are actually displayed depends on the site's approval and configuration. If advertising is served, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit or personalise advertising, subject to user settings, applicable law and Google's policies. Advertising technologies may process information such as browser identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and visits to websites or apps.

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate consent choices. You can also review and manage browser cookie settings. Changing browser settings may affect some website functions.

For further information, read Google's information about advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. External links

Neon Tower links to external services such as GitHub for project feedback. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control the information practices of external websites.

5. Children's privacy

The game does not request account registration or ask players to submit personal details to play. The site is not designed as a service for collecting children's personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of websites and advertising features.

6. Your choices

  • Clear this site's stored data in your browser to remove locally saved preferences and scores.
  • Use your browser's privacy settings to manage or block cookies and site storage.
  • Review Google's privacy and advertising controls if Google advertising is shown.
  • Avoid submitting personal or sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features, hosting or advertising setup changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Neon Tower GitHub issue page. It is public, so do not include private or sensitive information. A private contact channel is not currently provided on this site.

Important: This page describes the project's current intended practices in plain language. Third-party providers may process data under their own terms and policies.
