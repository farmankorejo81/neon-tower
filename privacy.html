NEON TOWER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Tower handles information when you visit and play the game. Neon Tower is a browser-based game. We aim to collect as little information as practical.

Information stored by the game

The game may use your browser's local storage to remember your best score and sound preference on your device. This information is used to provide game features and is not sent to a Neon Tower game server by the game code.

Cookies and advertising

Advertising may be introduced in the future. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is enabled, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, limit, or personalise ads, subject to its own policies and applicable law. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide advertising services. Advertising and consent options may vary by country.

At the time this policy was written, advertising may not yet be active on the site. This policy will be updated if the site's data practices change. For details about Google's advertising practices, review Google's advertising policies and technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies. We do not control how third-party sites process information.

Children's privacy

The game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself does not ask players to create an account or submit personal information.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data to remove locally saved game preferences and scores. You can manage cookies in your browser settings. If advertising is enabled, additional ad or consent controls may be provided where required.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the site changes. For questions or privacy-related concerns, contact us through the Neon Tower GitHub repository. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public posts.
